Binary wheels repaired with GNU Fortran may include libgfortran and libgcc
(GPL version 3 with GCC Runtime Library Exception 3.1) and libquadmath
(LGPL version 2.1 or later). The corresponding license texts are included here.
GCC source releases: https://gcc.gnu.org/releases.html
These libraries are separate from SALMON's Apache-2.0 source code.
